Programs and Events

Experience Uzbek Culture with UASH

We offer a wide range of programs and events designed to engage, educate, and inspire:

Cultural Festivals

Cultural Festivals

Celebrate Navruz, Independence Day, and other traditional Uzbek holidays with music, dance, food, and community spirit.

Language & Art Classes

Language & Art Classes

Learn Uzbek language, calligraphy, traditional crafts, and ceramic art in hands-on workshops.

Sport activities

Sport activities

Organize weekly soccer games

Plov & Cuisine Nights

Plov & Cuisine Nights

Explore the flavors of Uzbekistan with plov-cooking demonstrations, bread-making classes, and food-tasting events.

Educational Lectures

Educational Lectures

Attend talks about Uzbekistan’s history, architecture, and contributions to art and science.

Youth Programs

Youth Programs

Connect younger generations with Uzbek traditions through storytelling, games, and cultural mentoring.
